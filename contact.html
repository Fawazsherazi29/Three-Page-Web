How much does a website cost?

Most business websites start from PKR 150,000 and online stores from PKR 350,000. After a short call we send a fixed quote, so there are no hidden extras.

How long does a project take?

A typical five to eight page website takes two to four weeks from kickoff to launch. Larger stores and web apps take six to twelve weeks.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes 30 days of free support, and we offer monthly care plans for updates, backups, security, and ongoing improvements.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. We audit your current site, keep what works, and rebuild what does not, while protecting your search rankings.
