About Pixel & Ink

A small studio with big care for every detail

Since 2021 we have helped more than sixty businesses look credible online, load fast on any connection, and turn visits into customers.

Our story

From side project to full studio

We started as two friends building websites for neighbourhood shops after work. The projects grew, referrals followed, and in 2022 we turned Pixel & Ink into a full-time studio.

Today a team of seven designers, developers, and strategists works from Karachi with clients across Pakistan and the Gulf. We stay deliberately small, so you always speak directly to the people doing the work.

  • Fixed quotes and clear milestones
  • Weekly progress updates, no jargon
  • Free support for 30 days after launch
